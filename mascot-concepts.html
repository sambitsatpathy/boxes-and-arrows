Concept 01 · recommended

Boxy

A literal box from the lesson diagrams, given a face and tiny connector pegs. It is the course's visual vocabulary already — no new metaphor to learn. It hops on a right answer, winces and wobbles on a wrong one.

Reads as
“That box from the diagrams is cheering for me.”
Success
Springs in, eyes go happy, cheeks appear, three sparks pop. Settles into a slow bob.
Failure
Slopes in, brows drop, mouth goes wavy, one sweat drop. Wobbles once, then hides.
Placement
Bottom-right of the quiz step, ~20px off-screen, slides in and out in ~0.5s. Never takes a click.

The mascot stays for about 2.4 seconds, then slides back out. Try clicking both answer types repeatedly — each press restarts the motion cleanly.

Prototype only — nothing here is wired into the course yet. If you pick a direction, the next step is to add a sibling layer next to #app (so step re-renders don't destroy it), call it from answer() in app.js, and keep it CSP-safe with inline SVG and CSS only.
